IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters · 2024

Safety-Guaranteed Distributed Formation Control of Multi-Robot Systems Over Graphs With Rigid and Elastic Edges

Hoang Pham, Nadun Ranasinghe, Dong Le, Made Widhi Surya Atman, and Azwirman Gusrialdi

Volume 9, Number 11, pages 10240–10247

About the paper

This paper presents a distributed control method that enables multiple robots to navigate unknown environments safely while maintaining formations containing both rigid and elastic inter-robot connections. The method combines quadratic programming with control barrier functions, allowing the formation to adapt around obstacles while preserving required distance constraints and providing a safety guarantee.
