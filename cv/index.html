Profile

I am a Doctoral Researcher at Tampere University working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, robotics, multimodal interaction, and human-robot collaboration. My research focuses on developing intelligent, interpretable, and human-centric AI systems that support safe, effective, and intuitive collaboration between humans and robots in industrial environments.

My current work examines multimodal context understanding, conversational collaborative robots, cognitive validation, ambiguity-aware interaction, uncertainty estimation, and symbolic robot task generation for next-generation manufacturing systems.
